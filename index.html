Tour Packages

1. Bako National Park Tour

Enjoy natural beauty and spot proboscis monkeys at Bako National Park.

Duration: 2 Days 1 Night | Price: RM 300 / person
Includes: Tour guide, boat fare, lunch, and entrance ticket.

2. Mulu Caves Adventure

Explore world-renowned limestone caves and witness the spectacular bat exodus phenomenon.

Duration: 3 Days 2 Nights | Price: RM 750 / person
Includes: Accommodation, boat transport, and cave entrance permits.

3. Sarawak Cultural Village

Experience the rich arts, culture, and traditional houses of Sarawak's ethnic groups in one place.

Duration: 1 Day | Price: RM 150 / person
Includes: Cultural performances, entrance ticket, and lunch.

4. Semenggoh Wildlife Centre

An Orangutan Rehabilitation Centre in a forest reserve offering the experience to observe orangutans up close.

Duration: 2 Days 1 Night | Price: RM 520 / person
Includes: Entrance ticket and lunch.

5. Jong's Crocodile Farm

Witness live crocodile feeding demonstrations daily in saltwater crocodile ponds.

Ticket Price: Adult RM 30
Includes: Crocodile and arapaima feeding show, plus entrance ticket.
